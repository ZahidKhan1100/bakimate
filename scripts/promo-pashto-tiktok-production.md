# BakiMate — Peshawar Pashto TikTok Promo (Production Pack)

**Format:** 9:16 vertical · 50 seconds · 5 scenes × 10 sec  
**Dialect:** Peshawar / KP market Pashto  
**Platform:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts  

**Hook rule:** First 3 seconds = fight + text overlay so viewers stop scrolling.

---

## Global settings (use on every scene)

| Setting | Value |
|---------|--------|
| Aspect ratio | **9:16 vertical** (1080 × 1920) |
| Frame rate | 24fps or 30fps |
| Style | Realistic, documentary, South Asian shop — **not** Hollywood, **not** Afghan TV drama |
| Location feel | **Peshawar kiryana / Qissa Khwani–style bazaar shop**, Pakistan |
| Wardrobe | Shalwar kameez, casual — no formal suits |
| Skin tones | Natural South Asian, warm |
| **Avoid** | Kabul architecture, turbans as default, snow, desert, Western supermarket |

### Lighting palette (whole video)

| Scene | Mood | Key light | Fill | Practical lights | Color temp |
|-------|------|-----------|------|------------------|------------|
| 1–2 | Tense | Hard sunlight from shop door (camera left), 45° | Weak bounce from counter | Single bare bulb overhead, slightly warm | **5200K** daylight + **3200K** bulb mix |
| 3 | Shift | Same door light, softer | More fill on faces | Bulb + phone screen glow on friend | **5000K** warming |
| 4 | Hope | Soft key from door + **phone screen light** on three faces | Gentle | Phone UI glow (green/teal) | **4800K** + screen accent |
| 5 | Happy | **Golden hour** through door, backlit dust motes | Warm fill | Warm bulb | **4500K** golden |

### Camera language (TikTok)

- **Scene 1:** Handheld slight shake, medium close-up, both faces in frame — feels real, not staged  
- **Scene 2:** Slow push-in on note book (macro), then cut to frustrated faces  
- **Scene 3:** Stable medium shot, friend enters from bottom of frame (TikTok native feel)  
- **Scene 4:** Over-shoulder phone, shallow DOF, UI readable for 2 sec  
- **Scene 5:** Wide but still vertical — handshake centered, CTA space at bottom third  

### TikTok text overlays (burn in CapCut)

| Time | Text (Pashto) | Text (English sub, optional) |
|------|---------------|----------------------------|
| 0:00 | د بل پر جنګ؟ 😤 | Fighting over the bill? |
| 0:10 | نوټ بک خراب… | Notebook chaos… |
| 0:20 | حل لري! 👇 | There’s a fix! |
| 0:30 | ټول یو ځای 📱 | All in one app |
| 0:40 | ډاونلوډ کړه ✅ | Download now |

### Audio

- **0:00–0:20:** Low tense drone or muted dhol beat (optional)  
- **0:20:** Sound drop / whoosh when friend enters  
- **0:30–0:50:** Light upbeat acoustic or soft Pakistani pop instrumental (no vocals)  
- **Dialogue:** Record Peshawar Pashto separately; sync in CapCut
