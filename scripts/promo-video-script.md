## Production checklist

- [ ] Record screen captures at 60fps from a real iPhone (not simulator) — looks more authentic.
- [ ] Use a clean Malaysian-Indian shop background for the opening notebook scene.
- [ ] Music: mid-tempo, optimistic, no vocals. (Try Epidemic Sound — "Tomorrow Is Today" or any business-friendly track.)
- [ ] Captions burned in (most viewers watch on mute).
- [ ] Two cuts:
  - **60-sec** for App Store / Play Store preview (use scenes 1–8 trimmed).
  - **30-sec** for Facebook / Instagram Reels (skip scenes 2 and 7).
- [ ] Aspect ratios:
  - **Vertical 9:16** for Reels / Shorts / TikTok.
  - **Square 1:1** for Facebook feed.
  - **Landscape 16:9** for YouTube and YouTube ads.
