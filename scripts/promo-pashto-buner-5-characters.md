# BakiMate — Buner District Pashto Promo (5 named cast × 5 scenes)

**Cast:** **Zahid · Ubaid · Imran · Sudais · Rayees**  
**Format:** TikTok 9:16 · ~50 seconds · 10 sec per scene  
**Setting:** Buner bazaar kiryana
