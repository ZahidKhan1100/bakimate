# BakiMate — Peshawar Pashto Promo (5 scenes × 10 sec)

**Dialect:** Peshawar / Khyber Pakhtunkhwa market Pashto (not Kabuli / Afghan formal).  
Natural shop talk — note book, bill, balance, customer, WhatsApp where locals actually say them.

**Story:** Dukan owner and customer fight over the bill. A malgary walks in, shows BakiMate, life gets easy.

**Total length:** ~50 seconds  
**Setting:** Peshawar-style kiryana / general store, daytime

---

## Scene 1 — The argument (0:00–0:10)

### Visual prompt (Veo / Google Flow)
```
Cinematic 10-second clip, 24fps, shallow depth of field.
Peshawar-style small kiryana shop: wooden counter, rice sacks, biscuit racks, Urdu/Pashto signboards.
```
